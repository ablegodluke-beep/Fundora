Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Your privacy matters to Fundora. This policy explains the types of personal information that may be collected when you use our platform, how that information may be used and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, Fundora may collect:

  • Account information, including your full name, email address and phone number.
  • Account security information needed to protect your account.
  • Package purchases, payment references and transaction records.
  • Referral activity and eligible commission records.
  • Withdrawal details, including bank information you provide when requesting a withdrawal.
  • Technical information such as device, browser and platform activity needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage member accounts.
  • Provide access to affiliate packages and platform features.
  • Verify purchases, transactions and referral commissions.
  • Process eligible withdrawal requests.
  • Communicate important account and service updates.
  • Detect suspicious activity and prevent fraud.
  • Improve the performance, safety and reliability of Fundora.

3. Payments and Financial Information

Payments may be handled through third-party payment providers. Where a provider is used, payment information may be processed under that provider's own privacy and security policies.

Fundora may retain transaction references and payment status information needed to verify purchases and maintain accurate records. Do not share your password, PIN or payment authentication codes with anyone claiming to represent Fundora.

4. When Information May Be Shared

Fundora does not sell members' personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary:

  • With service providers that help operate the platform, process payments or provide technical services.
  • To verify transactions and prevent fraud.
  • Where required by applicable law or a lawful request from an authorised authority.
  • To protect the rights, safety and security of members and Fundora.

5. Data Storage and Security

Fundora takes reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access to personal information should be limited to people and service providers who need it to perform authorised tasks.

6. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permitted.
  • Object to or request limits on certain uses of your information.
  • Raise a concern about how your information is handled.

8. Cookies and Technical Data

Fundora may use cookies or similar technologies where needed to support account sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality and protect the platform. Browser settings may allow you to control cookies, although some features may not work properly if they are disabled.

9. Children's Privacy

Fundora is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal obligations change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact and Privacy Requests

For questions about this policy or requests concerning your personal information, contact Fundora through the official support channel provided on the platform.
